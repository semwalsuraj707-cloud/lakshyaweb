Defence Examination

NDA

Preparation resources for the National Defence Academy examination.

Mathematics English General Knowledge General Science Current Affairs
Learn More

About the exam: The National Defence Academy & Naval Academy examination is conducted by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) for admission to the National Defence Academy.

Commonly covered areas: Mathematics, and a General Ability Test covering English, Physics, Chemistry, General Science, History & Freedom Movement, Geography and Current Events.

What Lakshya provides: topic-wise study resources, practice questions, previous-year-question practice and mock-test style practice for this track, organised for self-paced study.

Refer to the latest official UPSC notification for the authoritative syllabus and pattern.

Preparation resources for the Combined Defence Services examination.

English General Knowledge Elementary Mathematics
Learn More

About the exam: The Combined Defence Services examination is conducted by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) for recruitment into the Indian Military Academy, Naval Academy, Air Force Academy and Officers Training Academy.

Commonly covered areas: English, General Knowledge and Elementary Mathematics (subject combination varies by academy — check the official notification).

What Lakshya provides: topic-wise study resources, practice questions, previous-year-question practice and mock-test style practice for this track, organised for self-paced study.

Refer to the latest official UPSC notification for the authoritative syllabus and pattern.

About the exam: The SSC General Duty (GD) Constable examination is conducted by the Staff Selection Commission (SSC) for recruitment of Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces, SSF and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles.
