About Us

Who We Are

Lakshya is an online preparation platform built to make competitive and entrance exam preparation structured, accessible and honest.

What is Lakshya?

Lakshya is an Indian online education platform that provides preparation resources for competitive and entrance examinations. The name "Lakshya" means aim or target — and that is exactly what the platform is built around: helping every learner set a clear goal and work towards it with a structured plan.

Preparing for examinations such as NDA, CDS and SSC GD can feel overwhelming. Syllabuses are wide, sources are scattered, and it is difficult to know what to study next. Lakshya brings together organised study resources, practice questions, mock tests and previous-year question practice in one place, so preparation becomes systematic instead of stressful.

Lakshya is operated as a sole proprietorship based in India. We believe in transparent, honest guidance: we provide the resources and structure — the effort and the results belong to the learner.

Our Approach

How We Think About Exam Preparation

Four principles guide every resource we build.

1. Syllabus-first planning

We organise preparation around the official syllabus areas of each examination, broken into manageable topics with a logical study order.

2. Practice-driven learning

Concepts are reinforced with practice questions, mock tests and previous-year question practice, because testing yourself is how learning sticks.

3. Self-paced flexibility

School, college and work schedules differ. Our resources are digital and self-paced, so learners study when it suits them best.

4. Honest guidance

We do not promise selections, ranks, admissions or jobs. We provide preparation resources and clear information — and we always advise learners to verify exam details in official notifications.
