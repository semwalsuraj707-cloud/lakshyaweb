Learning Features

Built Around How Learning Actually Works

Every Lakshya plan is designed around a simple loop: understand the topic, practise it, test yourself, and revise what needs work.

  • Topic-wise preparation — study the syllabus in a planned, logical order.
  • Practice questions — apply every concept immediately after learning it.
  • Mock tests — rehearse real exam timing and pressure.
  • Previous-year-question practice — understand how concepts have been asked before.
  • Performance analytics — see which topics need more attention.
  • Structured learning resources — organised material instead of scattered sources.
  • Progress tracking — know exactly how far you have come and what is left.

FAQ

Questions, Answered Honestly

Straight answers about how Lakshya works.

What is Lakshya?

Lakshya is an online preparation platform offering structured learning resources, practice questions, mock tests and previous-year question practice for competitive and entrance examinations in India. It is operated as a sole proprietorship.

Which examinations does Lakshya support?

We currently provide preparation resources for NDA, CDS and SSC GD. See the Exams page for details.

Is Lakshya affiliated with UPSC or SSC?

No. Lakshya is an independent education platform. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with UPSC, SSC, NTA or any government body. Examination names are used only to identify relevant preparation areas. Always verify official exam details from official notifications.

Does Lakshya guarantee examination selection?

No. Lakshya provides educational preparation resources only. We do not and cannot guarantee examination results, ranks, admissions, selection or employment. Outcomes depend on each learner's effort and performance.
