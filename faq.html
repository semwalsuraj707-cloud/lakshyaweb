Frequently Asked Questions

Straight, honest answers about how Lakshya works.

What is Lakshya?

Lakshya is an online preparation platform that provides structured learning resources, practice questions, mock tests and previous-year question practice for competitive and entrance examinations in India. It is operated as a sole proprietorship. See the About page for more details.

Which examinations does Lakshya support?

We currently provide preparation resources for NDA, CDS and SSC GD. Visit the Exams page for details of each track.

Is Lakshya affiliated with UPSC or SSC?

No. Lakshya is an independent education platform. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with UPSC, SSC, NTA or any government body. Examination names are used only to identify relevant preparation areas. Always verify official exam details from official notifications and websites of the respective conducting bodies.

How does Premium work?

Premium unlocks the complete Lakshya preparation experience for your chosen examination — premium learning resources, additional practice and test access, performance analytics where available, and structured, topic-wise preparation resources. Exact inclusions are listed on the Pricing page, and the billing model (monthly/yearly or one-time) is shown at checkout.

How do I purchase Premium?

Premium is priced at ₹99 — see the Pricing page. To activate it, contact us through the Contact page or email satyamrana719@gmail.com, and we will set up your access.

How is digital access delivered?

Lakshya products are digital. Access is delivered electronically to your Lakshya account after successful payment confirmation — there is no physical shipping. Details are in our Shipping & Delivery Policy.

What happens if my payment succeeds but Premium is not activated?

Contact us with your payment date, amount, transaction reference and registered email address. We will verify the payment with our payment service provider and either activate your access or ensure the amount is returned to you. See the Shipping & Delivery Policy for the exact steps.

How can I contact support?

You can reach us through the Contact page, or by email or phone — the current details are listed there and on the About page.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes — cancellation rules for your plan are stated in our Refund & Cancellation Policy. In general, you can stop renewing before the next billing date; the access you have already purchased remains available until the end of its period.

How do refunds work?

Refund eligibility, the request process and timelines are described in the Refund & Cancellation Policy. Duplicate, failed and accidental payments are handled there too. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method via our payment service provider.

Does Lakshya guarantee examination selection?

No. Lakshya provides educational preparation resources only. We do not and cannot guarantee examination results, ranks, admissions, selection, or employment. Your outcome depends on your own preparation and performance in the examination. Any claim of a guaranteed result, anywhere, is not from Lakshya.

Still have a question? Contact us — we are happy to help.
